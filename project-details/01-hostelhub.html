HostelHub Hostel Management System

About the Project

HostelHub is a professional hostel management system that makes student accommodation effortless. It covers room discovery, booking, resident records, and day-to-day hostel operations in one clean, responsive interface.

Main Features

  • Room browsing with live availability
  • Simple booking & reservation flow
  • Resident & occupancy management
  • Admin dashboard for hostel staff
  • Facilities & contact information
  • Fully responsive mobile-first design
