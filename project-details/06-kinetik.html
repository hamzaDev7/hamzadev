Kinetik AI Video to Clips

About the Project

Kinetik AI turns long videos into short, shareable clips using AI. Upload a video and split it by scene, duration, count, or custom range — with prompt-to-video storyboards and entirely browser-based editing.

Main Features

  • Upload videos straight from the browser
  • AI scene-detection splitting
  • Split by duration, count, or custom range
  • Prompt-to-video storyboards
  • Export shareable short clips
  • 100% free, browser-based editing
